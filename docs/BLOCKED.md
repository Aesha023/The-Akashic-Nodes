# Blocked Items and Known Issues

## 1. Resolved Backend Items
- **Piping Failure Orifice-to-Weir Transition [RESOLVED]:** Implemented in `core/pravahx/breach/hydrograph.py` via `progression_mode="piping_orifice_to_weir"`. Submerged expanding orifice equation ($Q = C_d A \sqrt{2gH}$ with standard HEC-RAS $C_d = 0.59$) routes discharge during initial pipe expansion; transitions to open-channel broad-crested weir flow when breach reaches the user-specified `piping_collapse_fraction`. HEC-RAS Manual (Ch. 14) quoted: *"While a coefficient for a true orifice is typically around 0.8, values in the range of 0.5 to 0.6 are suggested for piping flow to account for energy losses."* `piping_collapse_fraction` is enforced as a required parameter with no default unless explicitly specified. Verified by unit tests in `tests/unit/test_breach.py`.
- **Impact Assessment & GEE REST Endpoints [RESOLVED]:** Implemented in `backend/app/api/v1/impact.py` (UK Defra FD2321 hazard rating, population/asset flood exposure, economic damage estimation, evacuation clearance routing, disaster brief generation) and `backend/app/api/v1/gee.py` (GEE authentication status and Lake Watch satellite time-series analysis). Fully integrated and verified by unit tests in `tests/unit/test_api.py`.

## 2. Phase 2a: Hydrograph & Breach Known Limitations
- **Peng and Zhang (2012) equations:** Method not available. No accessible open source. `core/pravahx/breach/landslide.py` raises `NotImplementedError("Method not available: Peng and Zhang (2012) equations not available from an accessible source.")`.
- **Existing Lake Area-Volume Scaling ($\kappa, \zeta$):** Area-volume scaling power laws ($V = \kappa A^\zeta$) vary strongly by lake geomorphic type (e.g., moraine-dammed glacial lakes vs thermokarst vs tectonic vs artificial reservoirs) and region. Universal default coefficients cannot be assumed; `kappa` and `zeta` are required explicit inputs with no defaults.
- **Von Thun and Gillette Equations & Input Enforcement:** Re-verified formation-time equations and $C_b$ threshold storage table against HEC-RAS reference page; erodibility is enforced as a required parameter without defaults.
- **Teton Dam Level-Pool Overprediction:** Documented plainly in `docs/VALIDATION.md` that 1D level-pool routing overpredicts actual dam-break discharge due to lack of 2D reservoir drawdown and unmodeled breach channel headcut friction / tailwater submergence losses.
